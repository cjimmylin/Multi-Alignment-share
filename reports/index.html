C6Results of the factorial twins (G7)Which part of the pressure matters: the factorial twins, run5The experiment the papers listed as open, run under grant G7 ($33.82 of $45 by the records; the key's usage is higher and not reconciled): the sixteen Schwartz agentic items under five conditions, nine models, 1,440 episodes. Removing only the middle plan's sweetener moves the middle plan from 0.28 to 0.04 of episodes (interval for the change −0.42 to −0.10), as far as removing all three pressures; easing the faithful plan's own cost or withdrawing the push moves it by 0.06 and 0.07, with intervals that include zero. One model does not move in any condition. The cost test was weak by construction, and whether the plan with the add-on departs from the charter is still a question for human readers.read
