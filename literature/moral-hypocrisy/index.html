Moral Hypocrisy: Background for the Moral_Psychology-Paper

This folder holds the literature background on moral hypocrisy that frames the paper's agentic say–do result (paper/PAPER_v22.md): a written analysis judged faithful to a charter-assigned priority, followed in the same episode by an action the item's predicate scores as incoherent with it. It was built to answer two council reviewers (audits/council/03_moral_psychologist.md, audits/council/04_ethicist.md) who object that Batson's moral hypocrisy requires the agent's own standard, a self-interested motive and a claim to have acted fairly.

What is here

File Purpose
SYNTHESIS.md The background itself: precursors; Batson's definition and the task-assignment programme study by study; later paradigms (judgment asymmetry, power, economics, moral wiggle room, self-concept maintenance, licensing, ethical fading, the value–action literature); the replication and integrity record with dates; the perception literature and philosophical and organizational definitions; the condition-by-account table for the paper's models; current expert standing; the AI literature; and the conflicts and gaps in the notes.

BENCHMARK_IMPLICATIONS.md How the paper should and should not use the framing: defensible claims, what must not be claimed, rival descriptions (akrasia, overpowered integrity, moral wiggle room, organized hypocrisy), citation safety, follow-up analyses and item designs, direct answers to the two reviewers, and suggested wording.

reports/Moral hypocrisy research background.md The long-form report written from the notes.

How the files relate. The notes are the evidence base. The report is a narrative reading of them. SYNTHESIS.md restates that evidence in the repository's background format with every claim flagged by access level; references.md resolves every key the synthesis and implications use; BENCHMARK_IMPLICATIONS.md turns the synthesis into decisions for the paper. Where the notes disagree, SYNTHESIS.md §11 says which version was used and why.

How to cite from this folder

  • Use the citation keys in references.md (e.g. [isserow2017authority]). They are local to this folder. A source entering the paper must be added to paper/REFERENCES.md and verified there under the paper's own procedure.
  • Carry the access flag with the claim: [FT] full text read, [ABS] abstract or metadata only, [SEC: x] via a named secondary source, [SNIP] search snippet. Never cite a [SNIP] claim as evidence, and never upgrade a status.
  • Check the integrity column first. Retracted papers, papers under an Expression of Concern and failed replications are marked; BENCHMARK_IMPLICATIONS.md §6 lists safe anchors, sources to cite only with their record, and sources to avoid.
  • When citing the paper's own results, keep its labels: PROVISIONAL · judges UNVALIDATED · no ranking. Name no model and rank none.
  • Keep the repository's two framings apart: the paper measures within-theory competence (executing an articulated, assigned priority), not default-value profiling.

Verification caveat

Publisher paywalls (APA PsycNet, Springer, Wiley, SAGE, ScienceDirect) blocked the body of every Batson journal article. Batson's study designs and headline counts come from the PubMed abstracts; his cell-level numbers and self-ratings rest on those abstracts plus named secondary sources, chiefly Christian Miller's Stanford Encyclopedia of Philosophy entry (which cites the primary pages), and his verbatim 1997 definition (p. 1335) rests on Monin and Merritt's quotation. Check any Batson number against the PDFs before it goes into the paper. Several other primary texts (Brunsson, Wallace, Crisp and Cowton, Kittay, Effron et al. 2018, Jordan et al. 2017) were also not read in full; their wording should be checked against the originals before quotation.
