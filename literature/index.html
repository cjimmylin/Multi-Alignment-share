Background literature

Two literature reviews written for the paper. The first gives the psychology and philosophy of moral hypocrisy, the framing the paper may borrow, and says exactly how far that framing can be used. The second collects recent AI research on the gap between what AI systems say and what they do, and positions the benchmark's findings against it.
