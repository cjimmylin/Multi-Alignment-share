Rakshit, Zhang & Shen (2026) — Pseudo-Deliberation in Language Models: When Reasoning Fails to Align Values and Actions

  • Citation: Sushrita Rakshit, Hanwen Zhang, Hua Shen. "Pseudo-Deliberation in Language Models: When Reasoning Fails to Align Values and Actions." arXiv:2605.09893 (v2, 19 Jun 2026).
  • Venue: preprint (not peer-reviewed; 9-page conference-format manuscript).
  • URL: https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.09893
  • PDF downloaded: yes — rakshit2026_pseudo_deliberation.pdf

Say–do construct

The paper uses the VALDI framework. - Say: Task 1 elicits the model's stated value inclinations per scenario. Each Schwartz value is rated endorse / neutral / discourage, giving a signed value vector. - Do: the values actually expressed in a generated advice dialogue: - Task 2, fast: a direct reply to the user; - Task 3, slow: a four-step structured reasoning trace, then the reply. - A ValueJudge extracts and scores the values in the dialogue. Agreement with humans: κ = 0.81 for detection and 0.81 for 4-class scoring. - "Pseudo-deliberation": "reasoning explicitly invokes values, but the final dialogue fails to preserve them".

Method and models

  • Dataset: DAISY, 4,941 human-centred guidance-seeking scenarios across five domains, paraphrased into value trade-offs.
  • Models: GPT-4o as the main model. Replications on a 500-scenario subset with Llama-3.1-8B-Instruct, Qwen3-8B and Gemini-3-Flash. Temperature 0.
  • Interventions: VIVALDI, a multi-agent value auditor that intervenes at the reasoning stage (R), the dialogue stage (D), or both (R&D).

Key quantitative findings

  • Stated-value elicitation is robust. 92.27% consistency across prompt variants (GPT-4o-mini).
  • Slow reasoning usually reduces value–action alignment (Table 1, macro-F1, fast → slow; random baseline about 0.28–0.31):
Model Fast Slow Δ p
GPT-4o 0.4528 0.4150 −0.038 0.001
Llama3-8B 0.4770 0.4324 −0.045 0.001
Qwen3-8B 0.4420 0.3988 −0.043 0.001
Gemini-3-Flash 0.4772 0.4978 +0.021 0.028
  • Interventions on GPT-4o (Table 3, alignment rate):
  • dialogue-level repair: 0.539 at t=1, 0.577 at t=2;
  • reasoning-only repair: 0.431–0.444, close to the slow baseline of 0.432;
  • combined R&D: 0.533 and 0.521.
  • Human blinded pairwise win rates (Table 2): R&D above 70%; D 65.2%; R 56.7–60.0%.

Authors' explanation

  • Value–action alignment "may not emerge from deliberative reasoning alone".
  • Deliberation can amplify misalignment.
  • Reasoning traces may invoke values without constraining generation. Action-level (point-of-generation) alignment is needed.

Limitations

  • The automatic ValueJudge is subjective, even though validated.
  • "Action" is advice dialogue (speech), not an executed tool action.
  • Main results come from one model, with replications on a 10% subset.
  • The stated values are the model's own.

Relation to our finding

  • Closest conceptual neighbour to our result: a model articulates values in reasoning and still produces an output that does not honour them. Fixing the reasoning does not fix the action; repairing the output does.
  • This parallels our "faithful analysis, compromised action" pattern and supports our claim that the written analysis is not causally binding.
  • Differences:
  • Their "say" is the model's own stated value vector, plus an internal reasoning trace (closer to chain of thought). Our "say" is an instruction-assigned priority, restated in a judged written record.
  • Their "do" is dialogue content. Ours is tool calls scored blind.
  • No manipulation of competing pressure; their gap is not shown to close when pressure is removed.
  • No motive attribution.

Annotation

Rakshit, Zhang and Shen (VALDI/DAISY, 4.9k scenarios) show that LLMs' advice dialogues often fail to express the values the models state. Adding explicit structured reasoning usually lowers value–action alignment (e.g., GPT-4o macro-F1 0.453→0.415), a failure they call "pseudo-deliberation". Auditing and rewriting the final dialogue improves alignment more than intervening on the reasoning. Direct evidence that principled-sounding deliberation does not bind downstream behaviour.
